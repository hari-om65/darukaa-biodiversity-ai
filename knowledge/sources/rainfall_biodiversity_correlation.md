---
title: Rainfall Variability and Biodiversity Correlation
year: 2022
topic_tags: [water, climate, biodiversity]
---

Rainfall patterns strongly influence species richness and community composition,
particularly in seasonally dry ecosystems. Regions with stable, predictable
rainfall tend to support higher biodiversity than regions with high inter-annual
rainfall variability, since unpredictable water availability favors a smaller set
of drought-tolerant generalist species.

Shifts in rainfall timing and intensity driven by climate change are already
altering flowering, breeding, and migration cycles for many species. Amphibians
and freshwater invertebrates are especially sensitive, as their life cycles are
often tightly coupled to seasonal water availability in temporary ponds and
streams.

Long-term monitoring that pairs rainfall records with species survey data is
essential for distinguishing natural climate variability from human-driven
change, and for identifying which watersheds are most at risk of biodiversity
loss under future rainfall scenarios.
